Exam Workflow & Worked-Answer Structure

The tutorials and memos consistently expect more than a test name. A complete answer should show the statistical reasoning from the question to the conclusion.

1

Identify the test and justify it: objective, number of samples, data type, and independent versus paired/blocked structure.

2

State (H_0) and (H_1) in context.

3

Compute the statistic: show rankings, differences or rank sums where required.

4

Determine the rejection rule or p-value.

5

Make the decision. Compare the p-value with (alpha) (or statistic with its critical region).

6

Conclude in the language of the problem. Do not finish with only “reject (H_0).”

10.1 What your final answer should look like

Test: Name the procedure and justify it from the data structure.

Hypotheses: State both hypotheses in the language of the problem.

Calculation: Show the required ranks/differences and the statistic.

Critical region / p-value: State the rule before deciding.

Conclusion: Refer back to the original population or research question.

Final revision checklist

Can you identify the data type? Can you tell paired from independent data? Can you rank ties? Can you state contextual hypotheses? Can you calculate the statistic manually? Can you reproduce the test in R? Can you interpret the p-value in context? If yes, you understand the chapter rather than merely recognising formulas.
